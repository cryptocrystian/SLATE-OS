# PRODUCT.md — SLATE

> Product truth for the SLATE design system. Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md (written at finish), not here.

## What it is
SLATE is Saipien Labs' internal operating system for its AI consulting practice (the AdvisoryOps track). It runs the commercial wedge end-to-end: **AI Workflow Scorecard → lead qualification → AI Opportunity Sprint engagement → stakeholder intake → AI-synthesized findings / opportunities / roadmap → client report → tiered proposal → SOW**.

## Two audiences, two registers
- **Operators** (Saipien Labs consultants) — work inside the authenticated app (`/app`). Dark, premium, technical operator workspace. Mode: Operate.
- **Clients** (prospect/client executives — CFO/COO/CEO) — receive **deliverables**: the AI Opportunity Sprint Report, the tiered Proposal, and (internally) the SOW Draft. They read these to decide on an AI-transformation investment. Mode: **Read / executive**.

## T10 scope — the client deliverables (this initiative)
Rendered as print-quality documents + client-facing share pages:
- **Report** — `report-pdf-candidate-document` (operator/PDF, with `?mode=client`), `report/print`, public `client-report-share-document` at `/r/[token]`.
- **Proposal** — `proposal-candidate-document` (with `?mode=client`), public `client-proposal-share-document` at `/p/[token]`. Tiered options (good/better/best) with a recommended tier.
- **SOW** — `sow-draft-document` (internal-only, watermarked "Draft · not executed"; no public route).
Content: executive summary, findings, opportunities (impact/complexity), 30/60/90 roadmap, report sections (12 canonical), proposal options + scope + pricing placeholder + implementation-credit block, exhibits (Group-A charts: opportunity portfolio 2×2, priority quadrant, roadmap Gantt, capability maturity, stakeholder coverage).

## Non-negotiable product truth / constraints
- **Human-review gates are permanent** — AI drafts, a consultant approves before anything reaches a client. Nothing auto-final.
- **Client-safe rendering** — no operator/audit metadata (UUIDs, snapshot ids, claim-guard scan text, "generated by" initials) in client mode; snapshot-pure; the `?mode=client` sanitizer strips operator vocabulary.
- **Legal/commercial posture** — defensible non-binding disclaimers; pricing is placeholder/"validate before quoting"; SOW never public; no e-signature/payment.
- **Print fidelity** — client docs render in a light theme (`.slate-print-light` CSS-var scope) and must export cleanly to PDF (print isolation already handles browser-extension bleed).
- **Brand world** — Saipien Labs: premium, technical, trustworthy, creative. Operator app is dark with a blue-violet accent (~#6c8cff), Inter (sans) + JetBrains Mono (mono). Client deliverables are the light, executive expression of the same brand.

## What "done" means for T10
Client deliverables that read as **top-tier strategy-consulting output** — the kind a client would expect from a premium firm — not "stacked text blocks": a real cover, executive hierarchy, first-class data exhibits, tiered proposal comparison, confident restraint. Current state (docs/62 audit): Premium 2/5 — bare, single-column, no cover/letterhead/tiering. This initiative closes that gap.
