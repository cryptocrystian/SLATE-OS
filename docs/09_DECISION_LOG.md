# SLATE Decision Log

This file records important product, architecture, UX, and process decisions so future AI sessions do not drift.

---

## Decision 001: Product Name

**Decision:** Use `SLATE` as the working product name.

**Rationale:** SLATE feels premium, calm, architectural, and broad enough to support AI Systems, Custom Development, Venture Studio, delivery workflows, and future BuildOps.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 002: Product Role

**Decision:** SLATE is the internal operating system behind Saipien Labs’ discovery, strategy, build, and venture workflows.

**Rationale:** The product should support operations and also act as a proof point of Saipien Labs’ design/development capabilities.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 003: MVP Focus

**Decision:** The first MVP focuses on GrowthOps + AdvisoryOps.

Primary flow:

AI Workflow Scorecard → Lead Qualification → AI Opportunity Sprint → Audit Report → Proposal / SOW

**Rationale:** This is the clearest commercial wedge and supports near-term revenue generation.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 004: BuildOps Timing

**Decision:** BuildOps is important but deferred until after the GrowthOps + AdvisoryOps MVP is working.

**Rationale:** The development pipeline requires deep thought and should not be rushed. It will eventually support custom development, AI implementation builds, and internal venture builds.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 005: StudioOps Timing

**Decision:** StudioOps is a later phase.

**Rationale:** Venture studio operations are strategically important, but the first MVP must remain focused.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 006: Mock Data First

**Decision:** Use realistic mock data during early UI/product sprints.

**Rationale:** Backend decisions should not slow down visual foundation, UX flows, route structure, or component architecture.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 007: Human-Guided AI

**Decision:** AI outputs must support human review and must not become final by default.

**Rationale:** SLATE produces advisory and client-facing outputs. Quality, evidence, and human judgment are essential.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 008: Scorecard Boundary

**Decision:** The free AI Workflow Scorecard provides directional insight only. It must not include a full roadmap, technical architecture, ROI model, or implementation plan.

**Rationale:** The scorecard should create value and curiosity without cannibalizing the paid AI Opportunity Sprint.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 009: Visual Quality

**Decision:** SLATE must not look like a generic admin dashboard.

**Rationale:** The platform is also a sales asset that demonstrates Saipien Labs’ design and development capabilities.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 010: Website Continuity

**Decision:** The Saipien Labs website and SLATE should share visual DNA.

**Rationale:** The website hero will likely use SLATE-inspired visuals. The public scorecard should feel like moving from the website into an application experience, not a disconnected form.

**Status:** Accepted.

---

## Decision 011: Sprint Review Protocol

**Decision:** Every implementation sprint should produce a structured sprint summary, including changed files, implementation summary, commands run, test/build results, screenshots, deviations, known issues, and recommended next step.

**Rationale:** The lead architect may not always have direct repo/local runtime access. Detailed summaries reduce drift and enable effective review.

**Status:** Accepted.
